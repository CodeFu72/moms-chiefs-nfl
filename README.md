# Mom's Chiefs & NFL (`moms-chiefs-nfl`)

A zero-clutter, dementia-friendly standalone iPhone & iPad app built for Mom to check:
1. **🏈 My Chiefs:** How her Kansas City Chiefs are doing right now (Wins & Losses in plain words, division standing, last game result, next game date/time/TV channel, and full Week 1–18 schedule including Bye Week).
2. **📊 Team Wins & Losses:** How all 32 NFL teams are doing, with **AFC West (Chiefs' division)** pinned first, plus a one-tap **Best Record (All 32 Teams)** ranking.
3. **📅 NFL by Week:** The entire NFL schedule and scores week-by-week, automatically opening to the current NFL week with the Chiefs' game pinned at the top.

## How to Install on Mom's iPhone & iPad (No App Store or Mac Required)

1. Open the hosted URL in **Safari** on her iPhone or iPad.
2. Tap the **Share** button (`[↑]`).
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen** → **Add**.
4. Place the **Chiefs & NFL** icon in her bottom Dock. When tapped, it launches full-screen like a native iOS app without any Safari address bar, tabs, or external links.
